A BRIEF BIOGRAPHY

BIRTH, EDUCATION AND BEYOND

I was born on the 5th of November, 1988 in Ajeromi-Ifelodun LGA of Lagos state in Nigeria to a family of 8 in which which i am the forth child. Growing up in different areas of Lagos, I have had to attend a couple of schools for my primary education but one worthy of note is the Methodist Nursery and Primary School, Sabo, Yaba where I completed my Primary School Education.

I attended Mobolaji Bank Anthony Junior Secondary School in Sabo, Yaba for my JSS education and afterwards, I attended Lagos City College, Sabo, Yaba where i obtaind a secondary school leaving certificate in the year 2008 after sitting for and passing the West African Examination Council, WAEC exams.
