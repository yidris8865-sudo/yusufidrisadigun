About Me

Birth, Education and Beyond

I was born on the 5th of November, 1988 in Ajeromi-Ifelodun LGA of Lagos state in Nigeria to a family of 8 in which which i am the forth child.
Growing up in different areas of Lagos, I have had to attend a couple of schools for my primary education but one worthy of note is the Methodist Nursery and Primary School, Sabo, Yaba where I completed my Primary School Education.
I attended Mobolaji Bank Anthony Junior Secondary School in Sabo, Yaba for my JSS education and afterwards, I attended Lagos City College, Sabo, Yaba where i obtaind a secondary school leaving certificate in the year 2008 after sitting for and passing the West African Examination Council, WAEC exams.

In 2010, I attended Railway Technical College where I obtained a dipolma in Computer Engineering. Afterwards, having worked in an IT firm for about a year, I went on to attend Yaba College College of Technology where I obtained an Ordinary Diploma in Computer Science after a three years part time program.

In November 2017, I founded Valutech Gadget Solutions, a gadget repairs and sales company located in Computer Village, Ikeja which I currently run.
Due to my passion for and commitment to continous learning, I enrolled with MIVA Open University in May, 2026 to pursue a bachelor's degree in Computer Science and I am delighted to be undertaking this academic program with Miva Open University.

I love sports, I love people, I love God and I am passionate about Nigeria.
Thank you for your time.
